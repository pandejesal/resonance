What Resonance does

◈

Resonance Intelligence

Forgotten gems you rated highly but haven't played, rediscovery mixes for the last 30–365 days, decade journeys through your library, and sound-alikes matched on shared musical signals.

▮▮

Bit-perfect engine

A lossless-capable audio pipeline with gapless playback and crossfade, so your library sounds exactly as recorded.

≡

Equalizer & effects

A 10-band equalizer plus reverb, echo, and pitch effects — tuned per track, remembered per album.

⇣

Import from anywhere

One-click import from Spotify, YouTube, and Apple Music playlists. Your library, one place, on your disk.

≋

Waveforms & metadata

Scrubbable waveforms, a metadata editor for tags and artwork, and synced lyrics when available.

⛨

Private by design

No telemetry, no cloud dependency. Accounts, licenses, and analytics live on your server. Your listening history belongs to you.
