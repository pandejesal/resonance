Resonance on your phone

Resonance is a progressive web app. On iPhone and Android you install it like a native app — full screen, offline-capable, no store account needed. Your music still lives on your own server.

iPhone (iOS Safari)

  1. Open demo.resonance.app — or your own server's URL — in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow) in the bottom toolbar.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add in the top right. Resonance appears as a full-screen app icon.
  5. Open it from the home screen. It stays signed in to your server.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open demo.resonance.app — or your own server's URL — in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (three dots, top right).
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (or "Install app").
  4. Confirm the install. Resonance runs in its own window with its own icon.
  5. Done — no Play Store account required.

Notes

  • Same license — install the app on up to 3 of your devices and enter your Pro or Lifetime key once per device. The app itself is free.
  • Same server — every device points at your server. Playlists, ratings, and history stay in sync automatically because they all live in one place.
  • Offline — playback needs your server reachable (home Wi-Fi or VPN for your own server). The PWA shell loads from cache so the UI starts instantly.
  • iOS native app — we're shipping this PWA guide before committing to a native iOS build; revisit after launch data (see the changelog).
