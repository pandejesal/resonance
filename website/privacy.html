Privacy

Your server never phones home. Here is the complete, audited list of every connection Resonance can make — and the only things that leave your machine.

The promise

  • No telemetry. There is no analytics beacon in the app or the server, and no account is required to use Resonance.
  • Your library, listening history, ratings, playlists, and analytics are stored on your server and never leave it.
  • Resonance Intelligence runs entirely on your hardware — it analyzes your library locally and sends nothing anywhere.
  • When you pay, you pay directly. Resonance has no payment data of its own; checkout is handled by Dodo Payments.

The complete list of outbound connections

Audited against the source at v0.8.0 (2026-08-16). This is everything — there is nothing else.

WhenWhereWhat is sentDefault
Update check api.github.com — GitHub API for the Resonance repository Your current version tag, to compare against the latest release Off — you must enable "check for updates" in server settings
Lyrics lookup lrclib.net — the LRC Lib service Artist, title, album, and duration of the track you opened On demand — only when you open a track's lyrics
Buying a license api.dodopayments.com — Dodo Payments checkout Your order (product + success URL). Payment details go only to the payment processor. On demand — only when you purchase
Scrobbling ws.audioscrobbler.com (Last.fm) or api.listenbrainz.org Track plays you scrobble, using your own API key Off — only if you enable and configure it
Spotify import api.spotify.com Playlist requests made with your own Spotify token On demand — only when you import a playlist
Cast / multi-room http://<device>:<port>/cast Playback commands to cast targets Your local network only — never routed through the internet

What never leaves your server

  • Your music files and their metadata
  • Your listening history and play counts
  • Your ratings, playlists, and smart playlist rules
  • Your analytics dashboards
  • Password hashes and account data (all local)
  • The analysis behind Resonance Intelligence — forgotten gems, rediscovery mixes, decade journeys, sound-alikes

If this list ever needs to change, the changelog will say so before a release ships.
