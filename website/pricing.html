How licensing works

Do I pay per app?

No. The app is free to install everywhere. A Pro or Lifetime license unlocks features on up to 3 of your devices — enter the key once per device.

What happens when my Pro year ends?

Your license expires and you drop back to the Free feature set. Your library, playlists, and settings stay untouched.

Where does my library get analyzed?

On your server. Resonance Intelligence runs on your hardware and never sends your library or listening history anywhere.

Is there a trial?

Yes — every new server starts with a 14-day Pro trial, no credit card required.

Do you collect telemetry?

No. There is no analytics beacon in the app or server. We only know what you tell us when you contact support.
